# Design System: Premium Operational Aesthetic

## 1. Color Palette
- **Deep Graphite Black**: `#0A0A0B` (Main Background)
- **Cold Navy**: `#111827` (Secondary Background / Deep Surfaces)
- **Soft Carbon**: `#1F2937` (Borders / Accents)
- **Dim Steel**: `#9CA3AF` (Secondary Text)
- **Restrained Electric Blue**: `#3B82F6` (Primary CTA / Active Signals)
- **State Logic Palette**: 
  - *Verified (Stable)*: Soft Emerald (`#10b981`)
  - *Unstable (Warning)*: Dusty Amber (`#d97706`)
  - *Fragmented (Error)*: Muted Crimson (`#dc2626`)
- **White**: `#F9FAFB` (Primary Heading Text)

### Prohibited Styles:
- No Purple or Magenta.
- No Neon Cyan or overly bright "AI" gradients.
- No flat Gray backgrounds.

---

## 2. Typography
- **Headlines**: Architectural, high-contrast, editorial spacing.
- **Body**: Calm, high-readability, generous line-height (`1.6`).
- **Font Stack**: Inter, Outfit, or Roboto (System fonts preferred for speed, Google Fonts for premium feel).

---

## 3. Spacing & Layout
- **Section Padding**: `100px` to `160px` (Breathing room is luxury).
- **Max Width**: `1200px` (Standard) / `1400px` (Showcase).
- **Negative Space**: Used intentionally to draw focus to content.

---

## 4. Architectural Patterns
- **The Grid**: A subtle 20px grid pattern (`rgba(255,255,255,0.03)`) used in section backgrounds to reinforce "Engineering Discipline."
- **The Glow**: Soft, large-radius radial gradients (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.05)`) positioned behind primary visuals to create depth.
- **Topographic Lines**: Minimalist monoline curves used in the About page to represent "Flow" and "Philosophy."

---

## 5. Visual Density & Components
- **Borders**: Ultra-thin (`1px`), translucent (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`).
- **Glassmorphism**: Restricted to Navbar and specific overlays. Minimal blur (`12px`).
- **Cards**: Minimal usage. Prefer editorial layouts over repetitive grids.

---

## 5. Prohibited Elements (Critical)
- **No Dashboard Screens**: Avoid generic analytics charts.
- **No Glowing Everywhere**: Light should be a "signal," not a "background."
- **No Startup Clutter**: Keep text-to-visual ratio balanced and editorial.
