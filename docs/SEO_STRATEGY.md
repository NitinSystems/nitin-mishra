# SEO, AEO & GEO Strategy: Nitin Mishra Portfolio

## 1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
Traditional search optimization to ensure high visibility on Google/Bing.

- **Meta Title**: Nitin Mishra | CRM Systems & Workflow Operations Specialist
- **Meta Description**: Professional CRM architect and workflow automation specialist. Helping businesses eliminate manual chaos through structured operational systems.
- **Keywords**: CRM Systems, Workflow Automation, Marketing Operations, Lead Routing Systems, Business Process Optimization, Nitin Mishra.
- **Heading Hierarchy**: Strict H1 -> H2 -> H3 structure for crawler clarity.
- **Image SEO**: All assets will include descriptive Alt-text and WebP optimization for speed.

---

## 2. Answer Engine & Generative Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO)
Optimization for AI-led discovery (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini).

- **Structured Data (JSON-LD)**: Implementation of `Organization` and `ProfessionalService` schemas to help AI understand the brand's entity.
- **Clear Value Propositions**: Content is structured in "Problem-Solution" clusters to answer specific user queries about operational bottlenecks.
- **Consultative FAQ**: Designed with NLP (Natural Language Processing) in mind to rank as "featured snippets" or AI answers.

---

## 3. Social Graph & Open Graph (OG)
Ensuring the site looks premium when shared on LinkedIn, X, or WhatsApp.

- **og:title**: Nitin Mishra - Premium Operational Systems
- **og:description**: Scalable workflow architecture and CRM systems for high-growth businesses.
- **og:image**: A custom-designed social share card matching the Graphite/Blue brand aesthetic.
- **twitter:card**: summary_large_image

---

## 4. Technical Schema (JSON-LD)
We will include the following schema in the `<head>`:
- `Person`: To establish Nitin Mishra as a founder-led authority.
- `Service`: To detail the specific operational capabilities.
- `FAQPage`: To help Google display questions/answers directly in SERPs.
