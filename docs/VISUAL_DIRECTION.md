# Visual Direction: Editorial & Operational

## 1. Imagery Style
- **Workflow Screenshots**: Real implementation visuals from tools like Make, HubSpot, or Airtable.
- **Treatment**: Slightly blurred backgrounds, high-contrast UI focus, editorial framing with generous padding.
- **Tone**: Professional, clean, and organized.

---

## 2. Operational Visuals (Hero)
- **Concept**: "Signal Routing."
- **Execution**: Geometric nodes connected by thin animated lines.
- **Motion**: Soft pulses moving from left to right, representing data or lead flow.
- **Feel**: Like a living diagram of a business "nervous system."

---

## 3. Logo & Brand Assets
- **NM Monogram**: A minimalist, monoline geometric icon. The "N" and "M" are integrated through a single continuous line, representing "Workflow Connectivity."
- **Typography Logo**: "Nitin Mishra" in a clean, weighted sans-serif (Outfit or Inter).
- **Favicon**: A high-contrast version of the NM Monogram, optimized for 16x16 and 32x32 visibility.
- **Platform Logos**: Official monochrome SVGs only. No colored icons unless on hover.

---

## 4. Prohibited Visuals
- **No Stock Dashboards**: Avoid generic "AI" or "Analytics" screens.
- **No Futuristic AI Brains**: Keep the focus on *Operations*, not *Science Fiction*.
- **No Low-Quality JPEGs**: All images must be high-resolution or vector-based.
- **No Dense Grids**: Every visual element must have space to breathe.

---

## 5. Composition Principles
- **Asymmetry**: Use unbalanced layouts to create visual interest and editorial flow.
- **Overlap**: Subtle overlapping of elements (text over image) to create depth.
- **Scale**: Use large-scale typography for headlines to create authority.
