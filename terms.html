Terms of Service

Comprehensive operational boundaries and liability protections. Last Updated: August 2026

1. Scope of Services & Platforms

Nitin Systems operates as an independent systems architecture consultancy. Our services strictly encompass the design, logic mapping, and integration of automated workflows utilizing platforms such as Make.com, GoHighLevel, Airtable, HubSpot, Zapier, and OpenAI. We provide software engineering and consulting, not physical goods.

2. Statement of Work & Scope Creep Protection

Project parameters are strictly defined by an approved architecture blueprint prior to execution. Any requests for additional features, extra webhook endpoints, or scope expansions outside the initial blueprint will require a written Change Order and will be billed at a standard hourly rate or under a separate retainer.

3. 50/50 Payment & Advance Deposit

To secure calendar placement and cover initial discovery phases, a 50% advance deposit is mandatory. Zero logic is built and no workflows are executed until this deposit clears. The remaining 50% balance is due upon staging demonstration, immediately prior to live production deployment.

4. Accepted Payment Rails

To facilitate frictionless global operations, we accept payments via Wise (TransferWise), standard Bank Wire Transfer, and PayPal for international clients. Domestic clients within India may remit payment via UPI, NEFT, or IMPS. All invoices must be settled in the currency specified on the billing document.

5. Project Cancellation & Deposit Forfeiture

If a client elects to cancel or abandon a project midway through execution, the initial 50% deposit is completely non-refundable and will be retained to cover architectural discovery, time reserved, and initial logic builds. In such events, Nitin Systems retains sole ownership of all preliminary code and structures.

6. Deliverable Release & Title Transfer

We utilize private sandbox environments to build and verify systems. Complete ownership of custom scenario configurations, active production keys, and live deployment transfers to the client only after the final 50% invoice balance clears in full.

7. 30-Day Post-Launch Warranty

Upon successful deployment, we provide a 30-day post-launch warranty. During this window, any logic errors, defects, or misconfigurations strictly within the original approved scope will be resolved at zero extra cost. Post-warranty updates, maintenance, or new feature requests require an active retainer agreement.

8. Third-Party API & Outage Shield (Critical)

Our architectures rely on external cloud infrastructure. Nitin Systems explicitly disclaims all liability for system downtime, broken endpoints, token limits, price changes, or deprecated APIs enforced by upstream providers (including Make.com, Airtable, OpenAI, and HubSpot). Maintenance of active subscriptions is the client's sole responsibility.

9. Intellectual Property & Framework Reusability

Following final payment, the client assumes 100% ownership of their specific database instances and proprietary data structures. However, Nitin Systems retains full intellectual property rights to the underlying modular logic, structural methods, and blueprint frameworks, reserving the right to reuse these foundational assets for future builds.

10. Limitation of Liability & Lost Revenue Exclusion

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the total liability of Nitin Systems for any claim arising out of an engagement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the client for that specific project. Under no circumstances shall we be held liable for indirect, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits, lost leads, or business interruption.

11. Communication Channels Consent

To maintain professional boundaries, project communications are strictly authorized via Email, standard Slack Connect channels, LinkedIn Messaging, and scheduled Cal.com video calls. We do not provide ad-hoc 24/7 support via WhatsApp or direct SMS unless covered by an elite Service Level Agreement (SLA).
