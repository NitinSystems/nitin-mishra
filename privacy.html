Privacy Policy

Enterprise-grade data practices for B2B operations. Last Updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are & Data Governance

This platform is operated by Nitin Mishra, an independent systems architect operating out of New Delhi, India. In the context of global data regulations, we act as the Data Controller for information collected via our website, and as a Data Processor when configuring systems on behalf of our clients.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two distinct types of data: Voluntary contact information (name, professional email, phone number, and project scope details) submitted through our booking or inquiry forms, and temporary technical credentials (API keys, temporary logins) securely provided by clients during active development phases.

3. Zero Data Selling Commitment

Your trust is our operational baseline. Any information provided through our ecosystem is collected strictly for B2B project communication and scoping. We explicitly do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to external marketing agencies, brokers, or third parties under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Sub-Processors Disclosed

To facilitate seamless international operations and scheduling, data submitted on this site is securely routed through industry-standard sub-processors:

  • GitHub Pages / Netlify: For secure website hosting and continuous SSL encryption.
  • Cal.com: For calendar management and consultation bookings.
  • Make.com & Airtable: For secure lead intake routing and CRM record management.

5. Cookie & Tracking Transparency

This portfolio operates on a minimal-tracking philosophy to maximize speed and privacy. We utilize only essential operational cookies required for site functionality and basic anonymized analytics. We explicitly do not deploy personalized ad retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel) or invasive cross-site tracking scripts.

6. International Data Transfers & GDPR Rights

We routinely serve clients across the US, UK, and European Union. If you are a resident governed by GDPR or UK GDPR, you retain the statutory right to request access to your data, request corrections, or mandate the complete deletion of your records from our systems. Data processing occurs in compliance with international standards.

7. Security Measures & Credential Decommissioning

All communications run through secure SSL/TLS encryption. Furthermore, we enforce a strict credential decommissioning protocol. Upon the handover of any custom automation build, we mandate the immediate deletion of all client API keys, temporary passwords, and sensitive operational data from our local testing environments.

8. Direct Inquiries

For any privacy inquiries, data export requests, or mandate deletions, please contact us directly at: NitinSystems@outlook.com
